Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Trade Journal ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our web application located at trade-journal.ronniekhan77a.workers.dev (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in using Google OAuth, we receive the following information from your Google account:

  • Name — your Google display name
  • Email address — your Gmail address (used as your unique account identifier)
  • Profile picture — your Google profile image URL

Additionally, we collect and store the trade data you enter into the Service, including:

  • Trade details (instrument, side, result, amount, date/time, lot size)
  • Optional notes, strategy tags, and screenshots you upload
  • Custom instrument names you create

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your identity and provide access to the Service
  • To display your profile name and picture in the navigation bar
  • To store and display your trade journal entries
  • To compute analytics and statistics from your trade data
  • To isolate your data from other users (each user sees only their own trades)

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a Cloudflare D1 database hosted on Cloudflare Workers. Your trade data is associated with your email address and is only accessible by you. We do not share your data with third parties.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your data:

  • Authentication via Google OAuth 2.0 (we do not store passwords)
  • Session cookies with appropriate security flags (SameSite, Secure)
  • User data isolation — each user's trades are filtered by their email
  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission

5. Data Retention

Your trade data remains in our database until you delete it. You can delete individual trades at any time from the Portfolio view. To request complete deletion of your account and all associated data, contact us at the email listed below.

6. Cookies

We use a single session cookie to maintain your login state. This cookie contains your name, email, and profile picture (encoded). It is not shared with third parties and expires after 30 days.

7. Third-Party Services

We use Google OAuth for authentication. Google's privacy policy applies to the authentication process. We do not use any other third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

8. Your Rights

  • Access — You can view all your data in the Portfolio and Analytics views
  • Deletion — You can delete individual trades or request full account deletion
  • Export — You can view your trade data in the Portfolio table

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at ronniekhan77a@gmail.com.
